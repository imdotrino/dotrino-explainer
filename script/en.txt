Dotrino is an ecosystem of apps that builds security on trust between your devices.
Accounts are created automatically. Your information is shared only between trusted devices, and it never leaves them unless you decide it should.
To get started, choose the device you trust most: it will be your Vault.
Your Vault can be your own phone, your personal computer, or Dotrino can host it so it's always available.
Everything is end-to-end encrypted. No one outside your devices can see it, not even Dotrino.
